# Absence

Owns time away from work under the CLT: períodos aquisitivos, the férias ledger and férias
requests (reduction by faltas, art. 130; up to three periods with the 14-day and 5-day
minimums, art. 134; abono pecuniário, art. 143), faltas, afastamentos with the 15-day
threshold, the rules engine that encodes them, and holiday calendars. Periods for one
person never overlap, which the database enforces with exclusion constraints.

May depend on: People, Organization, Shared.
