# People

Owns persons (by CPF), their employments (one matrícula per company) and the employment
versions: each change to an employment has the period it is true in the world and the
period Sunex believed it, so the record answers "what was true on this date, as we knew it
then". Corrections add facts and never edit them. Also owns eSocial identifiers, admission
readiness, and reach resolution (manager chain and org-unit reach as of a date).

May depend on: Organization, Shared.
